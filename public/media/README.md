## General Rules

- Use lowercase filenames with hyphens.
- Use `.jpg` for photos, `.png` for transparent logos or QR codes, `.mp4` for video.
- Avoid fake stock photos, fake student testimonials or unapproved partner imagery.
- Prefer natural-light documentary images with clear education context.
