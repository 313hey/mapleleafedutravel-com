Maple Leaf Edu-Travel

A school-based way to experience China.

Founded in 1995, Maple Leaf Educational Systems operates schools and education programs across multiple cities in China. Maple Leaf Edu-Travel extends this education network into customized study tours, school immersion, Chinese learning and cultural exchange programs for overseas schools and partners.
