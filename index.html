Why Maple Leaf Edu-Travel

Built on education, not sightseeing alone

We help schools translate their learning goals into practical, age-appropriate China programs.

A Trusted Education Network

Maple Leaf’s education network spans multiple cities in China, offering established school resources and authentic learning settings.

Learning Beyond Sightseeing

Programs connect classes, inquiry tasks, student exchange, reflection and final sharing, so travel supports visible learning outcomes.

End-to-End Program Support

From program design and school coordination to local delivery, safety management and evaluation, teams receive structured support throughout.

About Us

Connecting schools with meaningful learning in China

Founded in 1995, Maple Leaf Educational Systems has developed schools and education programs across multiple Chinese cities and serves a diverse international learning community.

Maple Leaf Edu-Travel brings this education network into customized study tours, school visits, language learning and cultural exchange programs for overseas schools and education partners.
