## Replacement Notes

- Keep filenames lowercase with hyphens.
- Use `.jpg` for photos and `.png` for QR/logo assets.
- Do not use random online stock images in the final site.
- Check each image on desktop and mobile after replacing it, especially faces, campus buildings and landmarks.
